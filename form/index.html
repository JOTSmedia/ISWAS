04 — Form

Form

The subject is what the talent says. The rest of the picture supports that conversation.

This is not built as a host’s tour of the genre, and it is not built on reenactment. The picture is carried by sit-down interviews and by archival material: the films that started these careers, and the careers that followed, in the words of the people who lived both.

  • 01

    Sit-down interviews

    What the talent discusses is the scene. The interview is the record, not a bridge to another format.

  • 02

    Archive in support

    Archival material carries memory, context, and the work itself. It does not replace the person speaking.

  • 03

    The horror credit is the start

    The horror credit is where the story starts. It is not asked to be the whole career, or the whole film.
